Daisuke Iwai is a professor at the [Graduate School of Engineering Science](https://www.es.osaka-u.ac.jp/) at the [University of Osaka](https://www.osaka-u.ac.jp/), running the [XR Group](https://www.xr.sys.es.osaka-u.ac.jp/en/). His research interests include augmented reality, virtual reality, projection mapping, projector-camera systems, computational displays, digital fabrication, human-computer interaction, and Shitsukan science. Prof. Iwai is the recipient of numerous awards, including a [JSPS Prize](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-jsps-prize/) and the title of [Osaka University Distinguished Professor](https://www.osaka-u.ac.jp/en/guide/honors/odp), as well as many Best Paper, Poster, and Demo Awards.
